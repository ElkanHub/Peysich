The schools that struggle with fees are rarely short of parents willing to pay. They are short of a record. Money arrives on three phones, in two exercise books and a drawer, and at the end of term nobody can say who owes what. Here is a flow that fixes it without a bank, a card machine or a payment platform.

## One number, in the school's name

Register one mobile money number for the school, in the school's name, not the proprietor's personal line. Print it on every bill, every reminder and the notice board. When a parent sends, the name that appears on their screen must be the school's. That name is the whole fraud defence.

Money does not have to pass through any software to be recorded properly. It goes from the parent to the school. The software's job is the record.

## Every payment is recorded within the hour

Whether it came by MoMo or across the office desk, the bursar records it against the child the same hour: amount, method, and for MoMo the transaction reference from the message. That reference is what settles every "I paid last week" conversation.

In SchoolSpec: type the child's name, the amount owing is already filled in, choose **Cash** or **MoMo**, paste the reference, tap **Save payment**. The receipt prints and an SMS receipt goes to the parent.

## Every payment gets a receipt

Numbered, dated, with the child's name, what was paid, how, and the balance after. The parent gets one by SMS at once and a printed one if they are at the desk. Receipts are never rewritten; a mistake is voided and recorded again, so the numbers never have gaps and the auditor never has questions.

## The day closes

At the end of each day the bursar can see, by name, who collected what and by which method. Cash on the desk should equal the cash line. If it does not, it is found that evening, not at the end of term.

## Part payments are the norm

Plan for them. A bill of GHS 450 paid as 200, 150 and 100 over six weeks is a parent doing their best. Each part is a payment with a receipt and a new balance. The reminder that goes out shows the balance, so the parent sees the school noticed.

## What the proprietor sees

Two numbers, live: collected this term and still owed. Under them, the list of who is past due, oldest first. That list is where the office's afternoon goes, on the phone, kindly.

## Why not take payments online

You can, and one day you may. But a school that adds an online payment step adds a fee on every transaction, a settlement delay, and a reconciliation problem between what the platform says and what the bank shows. For a basic school where most fees arrive as MoMo or cash anyway, the simplest honest system is: money to the school's number, record within the hour, receipt every time. Do that for one term and the fee conversation changes.
