## Letters make sounds

Every letter has a **sound**.

The letter s says "sss". The letter m says "mmm".

When we know the sounds, we can read words.

Can you make the "sss" sound like a snake?

## Say the sounds

Say these sounds. Keep them short.

**s** like in sun. **a** like in apple. **t** like in top. **p** like in pot.

Say them again: s, a, t, p.

Which sound do you hear at the start of your name?
===
## Blending

Blending means we push the sounds **together** to make a word.

c - a - t

Say the sounds slowly. Then say them faster. Then say them fast.

c-a-t. cat!

You just read a word!
===
## cat

**cat**

c - a - t

A cat has soft fur. A cat says "meow".

Point to the letters. Say each sound. Then say the word.

Do you have a cat at home?

## The middle sound

Short words have a sound in the **middle**.

a, e, i, o, u

c-**a**-t has "a" in the middle.

p-**o**-t has "o" in the middle.

Listen for the middle sound. It is small but important!

## The -at family

Change the first sound and you get a new word!

c-at: **cat**

m-at: **mat**

s-at: **sat**

h-at: **hat**

These words are a family. They all end in "at". Can you read them all?

## pot

**pot**

p - o - t

A pot is for cooking. Mama cooks soup in a big pot.

Say the sounds: p-o-t. Say it fast: pot!

What do you cook in a pot at home?

## The -ot family

These words all end in "**ot**".

p-ot: pot

h-ot: hot

d-ot: dot

g-ot: got

Read them slowly. Then read them fast. Which one is the easiest to say?
===
## sun

**sun**

s - u - n

The sun is in the sky. The sun is hot and bright.

Say the sounds: s-u-n. Say it fast: sun!

Is the sun shining today?

## The -un family

These words end in "**un**".

s-un: sun

r-un: run

f-un: fun

b-un: bun

Sun, run, fun, bun! They sound alike at the end. Can you say them in a row?
===
## hen

**hen**

h - e - n

A hen is a mother chicken. A hen lays eggs.

Say the sounds: h-e-n. Say it fast: hen!

What sound does a hen make?

## The -en family

These words end in "**en**".

h-en: hen

p-en: pen

t-en: ten

m-en: men

Hen, pen, ten, men. Point to each word and read it. Which word is a number?

## dog

**dog**

d - o - g

A dog can run. A dog can bark. A dog says "woof".

Say the sounds: d-o-g. Say it fast: dog!

Do you know a dog? What is its name?

## pig and big

**pig**

p - i - g

**big**

b - i - g

The middle sound is "i". It is a short sound, like at the start of "in".

Pig, big, dig, wig. Can you hear the "i" in the middle?
===
## Slow, then fast

Here is the trick for every short word.

First say the sounds **slowly**: m - a - n.

Then say them a bit faster: m-a-n.

Then say the word: man!

Try it with: b-u-s, t-i-n, c-u-p.

## Tricky words

Some words are tricky. We do not sound them out. We just **know** them.

**the**

**a**

**is**

Look at them. Say them. See them again and again until you know them.

Can you find "the" on this page?
===
## Your first sentence

Now put words together.

**The cat sat.**

Read it: the - cat - sat.

A sentence tells us something. This one tells us what the cat did.

Read it again. Point to each word as you say it.
===
## The sun is hot

**The sun is hot.**

Four words. Read them one by one.

the - sun - is - hot

Now read them together. Does it sound like talking?

Is the sun hot where you are?
===
## The hen is in the pen

**The hen is in the pen.**

Read it slowly. Point to each word.

Where is the hen? She is in the **pen**. A pen is a small place with a fence.

What other animals live in a pen?
===
## A dog can run

**A dog can run.**

Read it: a - dog - can - run.

Can you run like a dog? Run on the spot! Now stop and read the sentence again.

Can a cat run too?
===
## Kofi is on the bus

Kofi gets on the trotro to go to town.

**Kofi is on the bus.**

Read it: Kofi - is - on - the - bus.

b-u-s is a short word. Say the sounds. Say it fast: bus!

## Big letter, full stop

A sentence starts with a **capital** letter. Capital T, not small t.

A sentence ends with a **full stop**. It looks like a dot.

**The cat sat.**

Can you find the capital letter and the full stop?
===
## Finger under the word

When you read, put your finger under the **first** word.

Read it. Move your finger to the next word. Read it.

Your finger helps your eyes keep the place.

Try it: The dog is big.
===

In school we read with the teacher. We read with friends. We read alone.

Reading can be quiet or loud.

Every page you read makes you a **better** reader.

## Words that rhyme

Words that sound the same at the end **rhyme**.

cat and hat

pot and hot

sun and fun

Say "cat, hat". Hear it? Can you find a word that rhymes with "dog"?

## Write the word

Reading and writing go together.

Say "cat". What is the first sound? c. Write c.

The middle sound? a. Write a.

The last sound? t. Write t.

**cat**. You wrote a word! Now try "sun".
===
## Make new words

Use these sounds: s, a, t, p, i, n.

Can you make **sat**? Can you make **pin**? Can you make **tap**?

Mix the sounds. Make as many words as you can.

How many words did you make?
===
## Read it everywhere

Words are all around you.

On the trotro. On a shop sign. On a tin of milk. On a bag of rice.

Look for short words you can read. **Stop** is a word. **Bus** is a word.

What words can you see right now?
===
## Read every day

Read a little every day. Even one page.

Read to your mother. Read to your little brother. Read to the cat!

The more you read, the easier it gets.

Reading is a gift you keep **forever**. Who will you read to today?
===
## You can read!

You can say the sounds. You can blend them. You can read cat, pot, sun, hen and dog.

You can read a **sentence**!

Give yourself a clap.

What will you read next?
