## Machines

Some things at home are **machines**.

A phone. A TV. A radio. A fan.

Machines help us. Machines need us to be gentle.
===
## A button

A **button** is a little thing you press.

Press. Click!

Something happens. The machine wakes up!
===
## On

Press the button. The light comes **on**.

On means it is working.

Say it: **on**!
===
## Off

Press the button again. The light goes **off**.

Off means it is resting.

Say it: **off**!
===
## On and off

On, off. On, off.

Mama presses the switch. Light on!

Mama presses again. Light off!

Can you say "on" and "off"?

## The phone

This is a **phone**.

Mama talks on the phone. "Hello, Grandma!"

The phone has buttons and a screen.

## Ring ring

The phone goes **ring ring**!

Daddy presses the button. "Hello?"

Can you say "ring ring"? Can you say "hello"?
===
## The phone is not a toy

A phone is Mama's. A phone is Daddy's.

We do not throw it. We do not drop it.

Ask first. Then be **gentle**.
===
## The TV

This is a **TV**.

Press the button. The TV comes on.

Pictures! Songs! Cartoons!

## The remote

The **remote** has lots of buttons.

One button: on. One button: off.

One button: loud. One button: quiet.

## TV off

TV time is over.

Daddy presses the button. TV **off**.

Now we go and play outside!
===
## The radio

This is a **radio**.

Turn the knob. Music comes out!

Grandpa listens to the radio every morning.

@image https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/1/15/Toshiba_Vacuum_tube_Radio.jpg/960px-Toshiba_Vacuum_tube_Radio.jpg | A radio | Masaki Ikeda · Public domain | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Toshiba_Vacuum_tube_Radio.jpg

## Loud and quiet

Turn the knob this way. **Loud**!

Turn the knob that way. **Quiet**.

Grandma says, "Too loud! Turn it down."
===
## Dance to the radio

The radio plays a song.

Kofi dances. Ama claps.

When the song stops, we **stop** too!
===
## The fan

This is a **fan**.

Press the button. Whirr, whirr!

The fan blows cool air. Aaah, nice.

@image https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/d/d9/Electric_Fan_240x355.jpg | A fan | Rfc1394 · Public domain | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Electric_Fan_240x355.jpg

## Fan safety

The fan goes round and round. Fast!

Never put your **fingers** in the fan.

Look with your eyes. Not with your hands.
===
## Fan in the classroom

It is hot in the classroom.

Teacher presses the button. The fan goes **on**.

Whirr, whirr. Everyone feels cool.

## The torch

This is a **torch**.

Press the button. Light!

Press again. Dark.

Lights go off at night? Use the torch!

@image https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/4/4a/Flashlight.jpg | A torch | KMJ · CC BY-SA 3.0 | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Flashlight.jpg

## Light off, light on

"Lights off!" says Ama. Dark.

"Lights on!" says Kofi. Bright.

Press the switch. Off. On. Off. On!
===
## Be gentle

Press a button softly. Click.

Do not bang. Do not hit.

Machines like **gentle** hands.
===
## Ask first

Before you touch a machine, ask.

"Mama, may I press the button?"

Mama says yes. Now press, **gently**.
===
## Wet hands, no!

Water and machines do not mix.

Dry your **hands** first.

Then you can touch the radio.
===
## Plugs are not for playing

The wire goes into the **plug** in the wall.

Plugs are for grown-ups only.

We do not touch them. We do not pull them.
===
## Save the power

When you leave the room, turn the light **off**.

When the TV is finished, turn it off.

Off, off, off. Well done!

## Not too long

A little TV is fun.

A lot of TV is too much.

When Mama says "off", we say **okay**. Then we play!
===
## Machines at the market

At the market, a machine weighs the tomatoes.

A machine plays music at the shop.

Beep! Mama pays GH₵10 with her **phone**.
===
## What machine is this?

It rings. Hello! What is it? A **phone**.

It blows cool air. What is it? A **fan**.

It shows cartoons. What is it? A **TV**.
===
## Well done!

You know a button. You know on and off.

Phone, TV, radio, fan.

Press **gently**. Ask first. Turn it off when you are done.

Clap for you!
