## Screens

A **screen** is a flat, shiny part that lights up.

It shows pictures. It shows words. It makes sounds.

Can you see a screen right now?

> Ask: Point to a screen in our home.
===
## The phone

This is a **phone**. It is small. It fits in a hand.

Mama talks to Auntie on the phone.

"Hello, Auntie!"

## What a phone shows

A phone shows **pictures** of people we love.

It shows songs. It shows stories.

It rings when someone calls. Ring, ring!

> Ask: Who do we call on the phone?
===
## The TV

This is a **TV**. It is big. It sits in the room.

It shows cartoons, football and the news.

We sit back and watch together.

## Watching together

Long ago, families sat **together** to watch TV.

We still do! Papa, Grandma, Kofi and me.

Watching together is the best way.

@image https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/9/97/Family_watching_television_1958.jpg/960px-Family_watching_television_1958.jpg | A family watching TV together | Evert F. Baumgardner · Public domain | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Family_watching_television_1958.jpg
===
## The laptop

This is a **laptop**. It opens like a book.

It has a screen on top and keys below.

Papa does his work on it. Tap, tap, tap.

@image https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/7/75/Laptop_in_the_living_room_%28Unsplash%29.jpg/960px-Laptop_in_the_living_room_%28Unsplash%29.jpg | A laptop | Oliur Rahman · CC0 | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Laptop_in_the_living_room_(Unsplash).jpg
===
## The keys

The laptop has many **keys**. Each key has a letter.

A, B, C...

Press one key. A letter pops up on the screen!

> Ask: Can you find the letter of your name?
===
## The tablet

A **tablet** is a big flat phone.

You touch the screen with your finger.

Abena draws a goat on the tablet. Swipe, swipe!

@image https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/5/54/A_child_drawing_from_a_Tablet%2C_Gombe_Nigeria.jpg/960px-A_child_drawing_from_a_Tablet%2C_Gombe_Nigeria.jpg | Drawing on a tablet | Shehuamin · CC BY-SA 4.0 | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:A_child_drawing_from_a_Tablet,_Gombe_Nigeria.jpg

## Big and small

The phone is **small**. The TV is **big**.

The laptop is in the middle.

> Ask: Which screen is the biggest in our home?
===
## On and off

Press the button. The screen goes **on**. Light!

Press again. The screen goes **off**. Dark.

Screens can rest. So can we.
===
## Ask first

Before I touch a screen, I **ask**.

"Mama, may I watch?"

Mama says yes, or Mama says not now. Both are fine.

> Ask: Who do you ask before using the phone?
===
## Screen time song

A little screen time is fine. Too much is not.

Sing along and learn when to stop.

## With a grown-up

I watch **with** a grown-up.

We sit together. We talk about what we see.

"Look, a lizard!" "What colour is it?"
===
## A little, not a lot

One song. One story. Then **stop**.

Kofi watches one cartoon. Then he goes to play outside.

A little screen. A lot of play!

> Ask: What will you do after one cartoon?
===
## Time to stop

"Time to stop," says Papa.

Kofi says, "Okay, Papa." He gives the phone back.

No crying. No fuss. **Well done, Kofi!**
===
## Turn off the TV

Turn off the TV. What shall we do?

Build a tower. Draw a picture. Pretend to be a goat!

Our heads are full of **ideas**.

@video C4mTDLt7Jw4 | Turn Off the TV! | Imagination Song for Kids | Kids Wyld
===
## Be gentle

Screens can **break**.

No throwing. No dropping. No banging.

Hold it with two hands. Gently, gently.

> Ask: Show me how to hold a phone gently.
===
## Clean hands

Sticky fingers make a sticky screen.

Wash your hands first.

No kenkey on the keys!
===
## No water

Water and screens do not mix.

Keep your cup **away** from the laptop.

Keep the phone out of the bath!
===
## Not too close

Sit **back** from the TV. Not too close.

Hold the phone away from your face.

Your eyes will say thank you.

> Ask: Blink your eyes. How do they feel?
===
## Screens are not toys

A phone is for grown-ups to call and work.

A laptop is for Papa's work.

We use them **carefully**, with help.
===
## Learning on a screen

Screens can **teach**.

Counting songs. Letter sounds. Animal names.

Kwame learns the letter K on a tablet. K for Kwame!

## Computers at school

Bigger children use computers at school.

They sit in a row. They type and click.

One day **you** will too!

## Talk about it

What did you see on the screen?

A dog? A song? A story?

**Tell** a grown-up. Talking helps us remember.

> Ask: What was the last thing you watched?
===
## Get up and move

After the screen, we **move**.

Walking, walking. Hop, hop, hop. Running, running!

@video fPMjnlTEZwU | Walking Walking | featuring Noodle & Pals | Super Simple Songs | Super Simple Songs - Kids Songs
===
## Play outside

The best games are not on a screen.

Ampe in the yard. Skipping rope. Chasing the hen!

Screens off. **Sun** on.

@image https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/b/b4/Ghanaian_Children_Playing_Ampei_02.jpg/960px-Ghanaian_Children_Playing_Ampei_02.jpg | Playing ampe outside | Tahiru Rajab · CC BY-SA 4.0 | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Ghanaian_Children_Playing_Ampei_02.jpg
===
## No screens at bedtime

At night, screens go **off**.

Grandma tells a story instead.

Then we sleep. Sweet dreams!

> Ask: What story shall we have tonight?
===
## Screens at the market

Screens are everywhere. The seller's phone. The TV in the shop.

We look. We do not touch.

Other people's screens are **theirs**.
===
## Screen rules

Ask first. Watch with a grown-up.

A little, not a lot. Be gentle. Clean hands.

Then **stop** and play!

> Ask: Can you say one screen rule?
===
## I know my screens

Phone, TV, laptop, tablet.

I know what they show. I ask first. I am gentle.

And I know when to **stop**. Well done, me!
