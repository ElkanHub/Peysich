## Big

Look! An elephant. It is **big**.

Say it with me: **big**.

Can you make your arms big like an elephant?
===
## Small

Look! A mouse. It is **small**.

Say it with me: **small**.

Can you make yourself small like a mouse?

Baby elephant is **small**.

## Ama's hands

Ama is three. Her hands are **small**.

Daddy's hands are **big**.

Put your hand on a grown-up's hand. Whose hand is big?
===
## Big ball, small ball

Kofi has a **big** ball. Abena has a **small** ball.

Bounce, bounce, big ball.

Bounce, bounce, small ball.

Can you find a ball at home? Is it big or small?
===
## At the market

Mama buys a **big** yam.

Ama carries a **small** tomato.

Big yam. Small tomato.

What is big at the market? What is small?
===

One **small** kenkey for Kwame.

## Tall

Look! A giraffe. It is **tall**.

Its neck goes up, up, up.

Stand up. Reach up high. You are tall!

@image https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/8/89/Three_giraffes_01.jpg/960px-Three_giraffes_01.jpg | Tall giraffes | Byrdyak · CC BY-SA 4.0 | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Three_giraffes_01.jpg
===
## Short

A goat is **short**. It is near the ground.

Giraffe is tall. Goat is short.

Crouch down low. Now you are short!
===
## Tall tree

This tree is very **tall**.

The grass is **short**.

Tall tree. Short grass.

Can you see a tall tree outside?

@image https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/e/e9/A_tall_baobab_tree.jpg/960px-A_tall_baobab_tree.jpg | A tall baobab tree | Awinsongyababa · CC BY 4.0 | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:A_tall_baobab_tree.jpg
===
## Who is tall?

Daddy is **tall**. Kofi is **short**.

Kofi stands next to Daddy. Daddy's head is up high.

Stand next to a grown-up. Who is tall? Who is short?
===
## Tall and short

Tall, short. Tall, short.

Reach up: **tall**!

Bend down: **short**!

Do it again. Faster!

## More

Look at the bananas. So many!

That is **more**.

Say it: **more**.

@image https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/a/a9/Bunch_of_bananas_on_sale.jpg/960px-Bunch_of_bananas_on_sale.jpg | More bananas | Wilfredor · CC0 | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Bunch_of_bananas_on_sale.jpg
===
## Less

Abena has one banana.

Only one. That is **less**.

Say it: **less**.
===
## More or less?

Kwame has three oranges.

Ama has one orange.

Who has **more**? Who has **less**?

@image https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/e/e9/Oranges_fruit.jpg/960px-Oranges_fruit.jpg | Oranges, oranges | Håkan Dahlström · CC BY 2.0 | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Oranges_fruit.jpg

## Water in a cup

Pour water. A little bit. That is **less**.

Pour more water. Up to the top! That is **more**.

Which cup has more water?
===
## More and less

More, less. More, less.

Open your arms wide: **more**!

Bring your hands close: **less**!

## Big spoon, small spoon

Mama stirs the soup with a **big** spoon.

Baby eats with a **small** spoon.

Find a spoon. Is it big or small?
===
## Big shoe, small shoe

Daddy's shoe is **big**.

Kofi's shoe is **small**.

Kofi puts his foot in Daddy's shoe. It is too big! Ha ha!

Can you find a big shoe?
===
## Pots at home

A **big** pot cooks rice for everyone.

A **small** pot boils one egg.

Point to the big pot. Point to the small pot.
===
## Big noise, small noise

A drum goes BOOM! That is a **big** noise.

A whisper goes shh. That is a **small** noise.

Make a big noise. Now make a small noise.
===
## Big step, small step

Take a **big** step. Stretch your leg!

Take a **small** step. Tiny, tiny.

Big step, small step, all the way to the door.
===
## Let's sort

Put the big things here.

Put the small things there.

A big cup. A small cup. A big stone. A small stone.

Can you sort your toys into **big** and **small**?
===
## Sun and stars

The sun is **big** and bright.

A star is **small** and twinkly.

Big sun in the day. Small stars at night.
===
## Mama and baby

Mama is **tall**. Baby is **short**.

Mama has **more** food on her plate. Baby has **less**.

Baby is small, but Baby is growing! Soon Baby will be big.
===
## I am growing

Last year you were small.

Now you are **bigger**.

One day you will be **tall** like Daddy and Mama.

How big are you? Show me with your arms!
===
## Say it with me

**Big** and **small**.

**Tall** and **short**.

**More** and **less**.

Clap for every word!

## Think about it
1. Can you find something big in this room? Something small?
2. Who is the tallest person in your house?
3. If you had two sweets and your friend had five, who would have more?
4. Can you walk with big steps, then with small steps?
5. What animal is bigger than you? What animal is smaller than you?
