## The alphabet

Letters make words. Words make stories.

There are 26 letters. Big ones and small ones.

Together they are the **alphabet**. Let us meet them all!

## A a

Big **A**. Small **a**.

A is for **apple**. Red, round and sweet.

Say it: "A, a, apple."

Ask: What colour is the apple?
