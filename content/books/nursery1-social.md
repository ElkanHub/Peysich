## My home

This is where I live. This is my **home**.

My home keeps me safe. My home keeps me dry.

Let us walk through it together.

> Ask: What colour is our front door?
===
## A house

Some homes are big. Some homes are small.

Some have many rooms around a yard.

Every home is **special** to the people inside.

===
## Who lives here?

Mama lives here. Papa lives here.

Grandma lives here. My sister Abena lives here.

And **me**!

> Ask: Who lives in our home? Let us count them.

@image https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/1/19/The_family_in_Ghana.jpg/960px-The_family_in_Ghana.jpg | A family at home | OfficialAlfy · CC BY-SA 4.0 | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:The_family_in_Ghana.jpg
===
## My family

Mother, father, sister, brother.

Grandma, grandpa, auntie, uncle.

Sing about the people in your **family**.

## The bedroom

This is where I **sleep**.

My bed. My pillow. My blanket.

At night I lie down. In the morning I get up!

> Ask: Who sleeps in your room?
===
## The kitchen

This is where we **cook**.

Pots and pans. A stove. A big spoon.

Mama stirs the soup. Mmm, it smells good!

@image https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/8/89/Kitchen_Stone_Stove_and_Cooking_Pots_in_Northern_Ghana.jpg | A kitchen with pots on the stove | Ibn Shiraz · CC BY-SA 4.0 | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Kitchen_Stone_Stove_and_Cooking_Pots_in_Northern_Ghana.jpg
===
## Hot! Keep away

The stove is **hot**. The pot is hot.

We do not touch. We stay back.

We help in the kitchen only when a grown-up says yes.
===
## We eat together

This is where we **eat**.

Kenkey and fish. Rice and stew. Banku and soup.

We wash our hands first. Then we sit and eat together.

@image https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/b/bc/Ga_kenkey_and_fish.jpg/960px-Ga_kenkey_and_fish.jpg | Kenkey and fish | Otuo-Akyampong Boakye · CC BY-SA 4.0 | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Ga_kenkey_and_fish.jpg
===
## The bathroom

This is where we **wash**.

Water, soap, a towel.

Wash your face. Wash your hands. Brush your teeth. Clean!

> Ask: What do we use to wash our hands?
===
## The sitting room

This is where we **sit** and talk.

Grandma tells stories. Papa reads the news.

We say hello to visitors here.
===
## The yard

Outside is the **yard**.

Here we play. Here Mama hangs the washing.

Here the hen walks about. Cluck, cluck!
===
## Everyone helps

A home has many jobs.

Cooking. Sweeping. Washing. Fetching.

**Everyone** helps, even small people like me!

> Ask: What job can you do?
===
## I can sweep

Kofi takes the small broom.

Sweep, sweep, sweep. The dust goes away.

Now the yard is **clean**.

@image https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/0/0a/Compound_sweeping.jpg/960px-Compound_sweeping.jpg | Sweeping the compound | Goodymeraj · CC BY-SA 4.0 | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Compound_sweeping.jpg
===
## I can carry

Ama carries the small bowl to the table.

Slowly. Carefully. Two hands.

"Thank you, Ama!" says Grandma.

> Ask: Can you carry a cup without spilling?
===
## I can hang the washing

Abena holds the pegs. Mama hangs the cloth.

Peg, peg, peg.

The sun will dry it. Helping is **fun**!

@image https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/5/59/Child_Hanging_Laundry.jpg/960px-Child_Hanging_Laundry.jpg | Hanging the washing | Johnnybam · CC BY-SA 4.0 | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Child_Hanging_Laundry.jpg
===
## I can fetch

"Kwame, fetch my slippers, please."

Kwame runs. Kwame finds them. Kwame brings them.

"Thank you, Kwame!"

> Ask: Can you fetch me something?
===
## This is the way

Sing it and do the actions!

## Tidy up

Toys on the floor. Shoes by the bed. Oh no!

Let us **tidy up**.

Toys in the box. Shoes by the door. Done!
===
## A place for everything

Cups go on the shelf. Shoes go by the door.

Clothes go in the basket.

**Everything** has a place.

> Ask: Where do your shoes go?
===
## The clean up song

Clean up, clean up, everybody everywhere.

Sing while you tidy. It goes faster!

@video SFE0mMWbA-Y | Clean Up Song | Kids Song for Tidying Up | Super Simple Songs | Super Simple Songs - Kids Songs
===
## Rubbish in the bin

Banana peel? In the **bin**.

Sweet wrapper? In the bin.

Not on the floor. Not in the yard. In the bin!

> Ask: Where does rubbish go?
===
## Water for the home

Papa fetches water. Ama holds the small bucket.

We need water to cook, wash and drink.

Do not **waste** it. Close the tap.
===
## Visitors

A visitor comes. Knock, knock!

We say "**Akwaaba**!" Welcome!

We bring a chair. We bring water.

> Ask: What do we say when a visitor comes?
===
## Morning at home

The cockerel crows. We wake up.

We wash. We eat. We sweep.

Then off to school in the trotro!

@image https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/3/3d/Trotro_boarding.jpg/960px-Trotro_boarding.jpg | Getting into the trotro | Amuzujoe · CC BY-SA 4.0 | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Trotro_boarding.jpg
===
## Evening at home

The sun goes down. We come home.

We eat together. We wash.

Grandma tells a story. Then we **sleep**.
===
## Rainy day

Rain, rain, on the roof.

We stay inside. Dry and warm.

Our home keeps the rain **out**.

@video LFrKYjrIDs8 | Rain Rain Go Away | Family Song for Kids | Super Simple Songs | Super Simple Songs - Kids Songs
===
## Be safe at home

No playing near the stove. No climbing on tables.

Tell a grown-up if something breaks.

Home is **safe** when we are careful.
===
## My home, my family

A home is not just walls.

A home is the **people** inside it.

Mama, Papa, Grandma, Abena and me.

> Ask: Who makes you laugh at home?
===
## Helping every day

Every day I can help.

Sweep a little. Carry a little. Tidy a little.

A little help makes a **big** difference.
===
## I love my home

I know my rooms. I know my family.

I know my jobs. I keep my home tidy.

I **love** my home!

> Ask: What do you love most about our home?
===
