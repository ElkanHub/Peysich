## Let us count

Ama has a basket. What is inside?

We are going to **count**. Counting tells us how many.

Ready? Point with your finger. Say the number. Let us go!

> Ask: Can you show me your counting finger?
===
## One

**One** goat stands by the gate.

One goat. Just one.

Say it with me: **one**.

## One of me

You have **one** nose. One mouth. One head.

Touch your nose. Say: one!

Touch your head. Say: one!

> Ask: What else do you have only one of?
===
## Two

**Two** mangoes on the table. Kofi cut one open.

Point to each one. One... two!

Say it: **two**.

@image https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/7/74/Mangos_-_single_and_halved.jpg/960px-Mangos_-_single_and_halved.jpg | Two mangoes | Ivar Leidus · CC BY-SA 4.0 | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mangos_-_single_and_halved.jpg
===
## Two of me

You have **two** eyes. Two ears. Two hands. Two feet.

Clap two times. Clap, clap!

Stamp two times. Stamp, stamp!

> Ask: Can you find two shoes?
===
## Three

**Three** oranges. Count them with me.

One... two... **three**!

Three is one more than two.

@image https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/e/e3/Oranges_-_whole-halved-segment.jpg/960px-Oranges_-_whole-halved-segment.jpg | Three oranges | Ivar Leidus · CC BY-SA 4.0 | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Oranges_-_whole-halved-segment.jpg
===
## Three friends

Ama, Kofi and Abena sit on the mat.

Count the friends: one, two, **three**.

Three friends. Three smiles.

> Ask: Can you name three people in our house?
===
## Four

**Four** legs on a goat. **Four** legs on a chair.

Count the chair legs: one, two, three, **four**!

Say it: **four**.
===
## Four of me

Hold up **four** fingers. Keep your thumb down.

Count them: one, two, three, four.

Wiggle them. Four wiggly fingers!

> Ask: Can you find four of something in this room?
===
## Five

**Five** fingers on one hand!

Count them: one, two, three, four, **five**.

Give me five! High five!

> Ask: How many fingers on your other hand?
===
## Five chicks

A hen walks by. Her chicks follow.

Count the chicks you can see. One, two, three, four, **five**... and more!

Cheep, cheep, cheep!

## Point and count

When we **count**, we touch each thing one time.

Touch, say one. Touch, say two. Touch, say three.

Never skip. Never count one thing twice.

> Ask: Count the buttons on my shirt.
===
## How many eggs?

Ama went to the market. She bought eggs.

Point and count. One, two, three, four, five...

**How many** eggs can you see?

@image https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/d/dd/Eggs_in_basket_2020_G1.jpg/960px-Eggs_in_basket_2020_G1.jpg | Eggs in a basket | George Chernilevsky · CC BY-SA 4.0 | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Eggs_in_basket_2020_G1.jpg
===
## The last number

Count the eggs again: one, two, three, four, five.

The last number you say is **how many**.

Five! There are five.

> Ask: Count three spoons. How many spoons?
===
## At the market

Tomatoes in a pile. Big red tomatoes.

Count five. One, two, three, four, five.

Mama says: "Give me five tomatoes, please."

Five little ducks went out one day.

Count them on your fingers as you sing.

One **less**, one less, until none are left!

## Count the claps

Listen. I will clap. You count.

Clap, clap, clap. **How many** claps?

Now you clap. I will count!

> Ask: Clap four times for me.
===
## Count the children

Children are playing in the yard.

Point to each child. Count out loud.

**How many** children are jumping?

## In the trotro

The trotro stops. People climb in.

One person. Two people. Three people.

Count them as they sit down. **How many** now?

> Ask: How many people are in our room right now?
===
## Count at home

Count the cups on the table.

Count the shoes by the door.

Count the pillows on the bed.

**Everything** can be counted!

> Ask: What will you count next?
===
## One little finger

Hold up **one** little finger. Tap, tap, tap.

Now two fingers. Now three.

Sing and tap along.

===
## Which has more?

Two mangoes here. Four mangoes there.

Count each pile.

Four is **more** than two. Which pile would you pick?

> Ask: Which hand has more fingers? Trick question!
===
## Which has fewer?

Three oranges here. One orange there.

One is **fewer** than three.

Fewer means not so many.

> Ask: Who has fewer shoes, you or me?
===
## Numbers in a row

Say them fast: one-two-three-four-five.

Say them slow: one... two... three... four... five.

Say them in a whisper. Say them loud!

**Always in the same order.**
===
## Count backwards

Now go down like a rocket.

Five, four, three, two, one... **blast off**!

Jump up high on blast off!

> Ask: Can you count down from five by yourself?
===
## I can count!

Kofi counted five stones. Ama counted five chicks.

Abena counted five claps. Kwame counted five jumps.

You can count to **five** too. Well done!
===
## Count every day

Count the stairs. Count the trees.

Count the spoons at supper.

Count your toes before you sleep: one, two, three, four, **five**!

> Ask: What did you count today?

2. Can you find three of something in our house?

4. What comes after four?
5. Which is more: two eggs or five eggs?
