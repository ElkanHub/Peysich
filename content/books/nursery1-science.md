## Animals near us

Animals live near our homes.

Some have four legs. Some have wings. Some have fins.

Let us go and **meet** them!

> Ask: Which animal did you see today?
===
## The goat

This is a **goat**.

It has four legs, two ears and two horns.

Goats walk along our streets in Ghana.

@image https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/2/21/Goat_in_Ghana_1.jpg/960px-Goat_in_Ghana_1.jpg | A goat in Ghana | Fquasie · CC BY-SA 4.0 | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Goat_in_Ghana_1.jpg
===
## The goat says

The goat says **meh-eh-eh**!

Say it with me. Meh-eh-eh!

Goats eat grass and leaves. Munch, munch, munch.

> Ask: Have you seen a goat eating?
===
## Baby goats

A baby goat is called a **kid**.

Kids jump and play. Boing, boing!

They drink milk from their mother.

@image https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/d/d9/Goat_kids_playing.jpg/960px-Goat_kids_playing.jpg | Baby goats playing | Abdellah Hussein · CC BY-SA 4.0 | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Goat_kids_playing.jpg
===
## The hen

This is a **hen**. She has two legs, two wings and a beak.

Her babies are called **chicks**.

Cheep, cheep, cheep!

@image https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/0/0c/Hen_with_chicks%2C_Raisen_district%2C_MP%2C_India.jpg/960px-Hen_with_chicks%2C_Raisen_district%2C_MP%2C_India.jpg | A hen with her chicks | Yann · CC BY-SA 4.0 | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Hen_with_chicks,_Raisen_district,_MP,_India.jpg
===
## The hen says

The hen says **cluck, cluck, cluck**.

She scratches the ground. Scratch, scratch.

She eats corn and little seeds. Peck, peck, peck!

> Ask: Can you peck like a hen?
===
## Eggs

The hen lays **eggs**.

Ama collects them in a basket. Carefully!

We eat eggs. The hen keeps some, and chicks come out.
===
## The cockerel

The father hen is a **cockerel**.

He wakes us in the morning.

**Cock-a-doodle-doo!**

@image https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/7/7c/Rooster_crowing_close-up.jpg/960px-Rooster_crowing_close-up.jpg | A cockerel crowing | Muhammad Mahdi Karim · GFDL 1.2 | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Rooster_crowing_close-up.jpg
===

## The dog

This is a **dog**.

Four legs, a wagging tail and a wet nose.

Dogs guard our homes.

@image https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/5/54/Dog_from_northern_Ghana.jpg/960px-Dog_from_northern_Ghana.jpg | A dog in northern Ghana | Hidrash · CC BY-SA 4.0 | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Dog_from_northern_Ghana.jpg
===
## The dog says

The dog says **woof, woof**!

Say it loud. Woof!

Dogs eat meat and bones. They drink water. Lap, lap, lap.

> Ask: What does a dog do when it is happy?
===
## Be kind to dogs

Do not pull a dog's tail.

Do not run from a dog you do not know.

Ask a grown-up first. Then stroke **gently**.
===
## The cat

This is a **cat**.

Soft fur, long whiskers, a long tail.

Cats like to sleep in the sun.

@image https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/4/4d/Cat_November_2010-1a.jpg/960px-Cat_November_2010-1a.jpg | A cat | Alvesgaspar · CC BY-SA 3.0 | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Cat_November_2010-1a.jpg
===
## The cat says

The cat says **meow**.

When it is happy it goes **purr, purr, purr**.

Cats eat fish and small bits of meat. They catch mice!

> Ask: Can you purr like a cat?
===
## The fish

This is a **fish**. It is a tilapia.

It has fins and a tail. No legs at all!

Fish live in water.

@image https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/8/83/Tilapia_Fish.jpg/960px-Tilapia_Fish.jpg | A tilapia | Nana Pearls · CC0 | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Tilapia_Fish.jpg
===
## The fish is quiet

The fish makes **no sound**. It opens and closes its mouth.

Do it with me. Open, close. Open, close.

Fish eat tiny plants and tiny bugs in the water.

> Ask: Can you swim like a fish with your hands?
===
## The lizard

This is a **lizard**. An agama.

It has four short legs and a long tail.

It runs up walls and sits in the sun.

@image https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/5/57/Agama_lizard_%28male%29%2C_Ghana.jpg/960px-Agama_lizard_%28male%29%2C_Ghana.jpg | An agama lizard in Ghana | ZSM · CC BY-SA 4.0 | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Agama_lizard_(male),_Ghana.jpg
===
## The lizard nods

The lizard is quiet too. But it **nods** its head.

Nod, nod, nod.

Lizards eat flies and ants. Snap!

> Ask: Can you nod like a lizard?
===

## Legs

Goat: **four** legs. Dog: four. Cat: four. Lizard: four.

Hen: **two** legs.

Fish: **no** legs!

> Ask: How many legs do you have?
===
## Covering

The goat has hair. The cat has fur.

The hen has **feathers**.

The fish has **scales**. The lizard has scales too.

> Ask: What do you have? Hair or feathers?
===
## Where do they live?

The goat sleeps in a pen. The hen sleeps in a coop.

The dog sleeps by the door.

The fish lives in the river. The lizard lives on the wall.
===
## What do they eat?

Goat: grass. Hen: corn. Dog: meat.

Cat: fish. Fish: tiny plants. Lizard: flies.

Every animal needs **food**, just like you.

> Ask: What did you eat today?
===
## Water

All animals need **water**.

The goat drinks. The dog laps. The hen sips.

Kofi fills the dog's bowl every morning.
===
## I have a pet

Some animals live with us. We **care** for them.

We give them food. We give them water. We are gentle.

## Big and small

The goat is **big**. The chick is **small**.

The dog is big. The lizard is small.

> Ask: Which is bigger, a cat or a hen?
===
## Move like an animal

Walk like a goat. Clip, clop.

Flap like a hen. Flap, flap.

Wag like a dog. Stretch like a cat. Wriggle like a fish!
===
## Guess the animal

It says meow. It has whiskers. What is it?

It lays eggs. It says cluck. What is it?

It has no legs. It swims. What is it?

> Ask: Can you make one up for me?
===
