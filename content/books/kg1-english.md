## Letters make sounds

Every letter has a sound.

When we know the sounds, we can read words!

Ama and Kofi are learning their sounds at school. You can learn them too.

Listen. Say. **Sound** it out!

## The sound s

**s** says *sss*, like a snake. Sss! Sss!

Wiggle your arm like a snake and say *sss*.

Sun. Sock. Snake. Soup.

They all start with *sss*. Can you hear it?

## Find the s sound

Kofi sees the **sun**. Sss-un.

Abena puts on a **sock**. Sss-ock.

Which of these starts with *sss*? Sun, cat, sock.

Say each word slowly. Listen for the snake sound.

## The sound a

**a** says *a*, like in apple. A! A! A!

Pretend ants are on your arm. Wiggle them off and say *a, a, a*!

Apple. Ant. Ama. Axe.

Say *a* three times.

## Find the a sound

Here is an **ant**. A-nt.

Ama's name starts with *a* too. A-ma!

Say the sound at the start: ant, apple, Ama.

Can you hear *a* in all of them?

## The sound t

**t** says *t*, like a ticking clock. T! T! T!

Turn your head side to side like a clock: *t, t, t*.

Table. Tin. Top. Toe.

Tap your toe and say *t*.
===
## Find the t sound

Kofi sits at the **table**. T-able.

He has ten **toes**. T-oes.

Which starts with *t*? Top, sun, tin.

Tap the table every time you hear *t*.
===
## The sound p

**p** says *p*, like blowing out a candle. P! P! P!

Hold up a finger like a candle. Blow it out: *p, p, p*!

Pot. Pin. Pen. Pawpaw.

Can you say *p* softly?

@image https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/4/46/Cooking_pot_for_Kokonte.jpg/960px-Cooking_pot_for_Kokonte.jpg | Pot. P-p-pot! | Agoetee 123 · CC BY-SA 4.0 | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Cooking_pot_for_Kokonte.jpg
===
## Find the p sound

Mama cooks in a **pot**. P-ot.

Abena has a **pen**. P-en.

Which starts with *p*? Pin, pot, ant.

Pat your knees for each *p* you hear.
===
## The sound i

**i** says *i*, like in ink. I! I! I!

Pretend to be a little mouse: *i, i, i*.

Ink. In. Insect. Igloo.

It is a small, quick sound. Say it fast!
===
## Find the i sound

Kwame puts the ball **in** the box. I-n.

Spilt **ink** is messy! I-nk.

Which starts with *i*? Ink, sun, in.

Point to your eye. That is a different sound. Ink starts with *i*!
===
## The sound n

**n** says *nnn*, like a noisy aeroplane. Nnn!

Stretch your arms out and fly: *nnn, nnn*.

Nut. Net. Nose. Nine.

Touch your nose and say *nnn*.

## Find the n sound

Kofi has a **nose**. Nnn-ose.

He is **nine** years old... no, he is five! Nnn-ine.

Which starts with *nnn*? Net, pot, nut.

Fly your arms for each *nnn*.
===
## The sound m

**m** says *mmm*, like yummy food. Mmm!

Rub your tummy and say *mmm*. Kenkey is yummy, mmm!

Mat. Man. Mango. Mama.

Say *mmm* and smile.

## Find the m sound

Ama sleeps on a **mat**. Mmm-at.

She eats a **mango**. Mmm-ango.

Which starts with *mmm*? Mango, sun, man.

Rub your tummy for each *mmm*.
===
## The sound d

**d** says *d*, like a drum. D! D! D!

Beat a drum with your hands: *d, d, d*.

Drum. Dog. Door. Dad.

Can you beat the drum ten times?

## Find the d sound

Kofi opens the **door**. D-oor.

**Dad** is home! D-ad.

Which starts with *d*? Dog, mat, drum.

Beat the drum for each *d*.

## Say all the sounds

Now you know eight sounds!

**s a t p i n m d**

Say them all with their actions.

Sss (snake), a (ants), t (clock), p (candle), i (mouse), nnn (aeroplane), mmm (yummy), d (drum).
===
## Sounds join to make words

Letters are like friends. They hold hands to make words.

Say each sound. Then say them fast together.

**s-a-t** ... sat!

This is called **blending**.

@video _ovFXpsWmS0 | Phonics - Learn to Read | Three Letter Words | Alphablocks | Alphablocks
===
## Blend: sat

**s - a - t**

Say it slowly: sss... a... t.

Now say it fast: **sat**!

Ama sat on the mat. Can you sit? Now you sat!
===
## Blend: pin

**p - i - n**

Say it slowly: p... i... nnn.

Now say it fast: **pin**!

A pin is small and sharp. Be careful with pins.
===
## Blend: mat

**m - a - t**

Say it slowly: mmm... a... t.

Now say it fast: **mat**!

Kofi sleeps on a mat. What do you sleep on?
===
## Blend: tap

**t - a - p**

Say it slowly: t... a... p.

Now say it fast: **tap**!

Water comes from the tap. Tap the table three times.
===
## Blend: nap

**n - a - p**

Say it slowly: nnn... a... p.

Now say it fast: **nap**!

A nap is a short sleep. The cat has a nap in the sun.

@image https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/c/c1/Six_weeks_old_cat_%28aka%29.jpg/960px-Six_weeks_old_cat_%28aka%29.jpg | A kitten having a nap | André Karwath aka Aka · CC BY-SA 2.5 | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Six_weeks_old_cat_(aka).jpg
===
## Blend: dad

**d - a - d**

Say it slowly: d... a... d.

Now say it fast: **dad**!

Dad starts with *d* and ends with *d*. Both the same!
===
## Blend: tin

**t - i - n**

Say it slowly: t... i... nnn.

Now say it fast: **tin**!

Mama keeps sugar in a tin. What is in your tin?
===
## Blend: map

**m - a - p**

Say it slowly: mmm... a... p.

Now say it fast: **map**!

A map shows us where places are. Ghana is on the map!
===
## Which word?

Listen to the sounds. Say the word.

**s-i-t** ... ?

**p-a-n** ... ?

**d-i-p** ... ?

Did you get sit, pan and dip? Well done!
===
## First sound game

A grown-up says a word. You say the first sound.

Sun? *Sss!*

Mango? *Mmm!*

Drum? *D!*

Now try: tin, pot, ant, nut.
===
## You can read!

You know eight sounds: s, a, t, p, i, n, m, d.

You can **blend** them into words.

Sat. Pin. Mat. Tap. Dad. Tin. Map.

That is reading! Keep going.
