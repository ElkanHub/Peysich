## My school

This is my **school**. I come here to learn and to play.

I have a teacher. I have friends. I have a classroom.

Ask: What is the name of your school?
===
## Getting ready

Kofi wakes up early. He washes. He puts on his uniform.

Shirt, shorts, socks, shoes. Bag on his back.

Ready for **school**!

Ask: What colour is your uniform?

## Going to school

Some children walk. Some ride in a trotro. Some come on a bicycle.

Ama holds Mama's hand on the way.

Ask: How do you get to **school**?
===
## Good morning, teacher!

"Good morning, class!"

"Good morning, **teacher**!"

We say hello when we arrive. It is polite, and it makes everyone smile.

## My teacher

My **teacher** helps me learn. She reads stories. She sings songs.

She shows me letters and numbers.

When I do not understand, I ask her.

Ask: What is your teacher's name?

## My classroom

This is my **classroom**. There are tables and chairs.

There is a board. There are pictures on the wall.

Ask: What can you see in your classroom?

## My classmates

Abena sits next to Kwame. Kofi sits next to Ama.

We are **classmates**. We learn together. We play together.

Ask: Who sits next to you at school?
===
## Making friends

"Hello, my name is Ama. What is your name?"

"My name is Kwame."

Now they are **friends**! It is easy.

Ask: How do you make a new friend?
===
## The bell

Ding, ding, ding!

The **bell** rings. It tells us what time it is.

Time to come in. Time to play. Time to go home.

@image https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/6/6d/School_bell_2.jpg/960px-School_bell_2.jpg | A school bell | Fotokannan · CC BY-SA 3.0 | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:School_bell_2.jpg

## When the bell rings

When the **bell** rings, we stop. We listen.

We line up quietly. We walk, we do not run.

Ask: What do you do when the bell rings?
===
## Story time

We sit on the mat. Teacher opens a big book.

We listen to the **story**. Shh! No talking.

Ask: What is your favourite story?

## Play time

Ding, ding! The bell says **play**!

We run outside. We skip. We play ampe. We play football.

Ask: What do you like to play at break?

## Taking turns

One ball. Many children.

Kofi kicks. Then Abena kicks. Then Kwame.

We **take turns**. Everyone gets a go.

Ask: Is it fair if one person plays all the time?
===
## Sharing

Ama has two biscuits. Kwame has none.

Ama gives one to Kwame. "Thank you, Ama!"

That is **sharing**. Sharing makes two people happy.

## Snack time

We wash our hands. We sit down. We eat.

Rice, banku, fruit. Yum!

We do not grab. We say "please" and "thank you".

## Clean hands

Before we eat, we **wash our hands**. Soap and water.

Rub, rub, rub. Rinse. Dry.

Clean hands keep us healthy.

## Being kind

Abena falls down. Kofi helps her up.

"Are you alright?" he asks.

That is being **kind**. Kind words. Kind hands.

Ask: How can you be kind to a friend today?

## Kind words

"Please." "Thank you." "Sorry." "Well done!"

These are **kind words**. They make people smile.

Ask: Can you say all four kind words?
===
## When someone is sad

Kwame is crying. He misses his mama.

Ama sits with him. She holds his hand.

Soon Kwame feels better. Friends **help** each other.
===
## School rules

Every school has **rules**. Rules keep us safe and happy.

Listen to the teacher. Walk, do not run. Use kind hands.

Ask: What are the rules in your class?

@video iQxK-Ah7has | I Can Follow the Rules | Learn the Rules | Learning Song for Kids | Preschool & Kindergarten | HeidiSongs
===
## Rule one: listen

When the teacher talks, we **listen**.

Eyes on the teacher. Mouth closed. Ears open.

Ask: Show me your listening face!
===
## Rule two: hands up

Do you want to speak? Put your **hand up**.

Wait for the teacher to say your name.

Then speak. Everyone gets a turn.
===
## Rule three: kind hands

Hands are for helping. Hands are for clapping.

Hands are not for hitting. Hands are not for pushing.

We use **kind hands**.
===
## Rule four: tidy up

Toys out? Toys back!

Books out? Books back!

We **tidy up** our classroom. It is everyone's job.

Ask: Where do the crayons go when we finish?
===
## Saying sorry

Kofi bumps Abena. It was a mistake.

"**Sorry**, Abena."

"It's okay, Kofi."

Sorry fixes things. Then we are friends again.
===
## Helping at school

We can help the teacher. Carry the books. Clean the board.

We can help our friends. Share a crayon. Hold the door.

**Helpers** make school better.

Ask: How did you help today?
===
## Home time

Ding, ding! The last **bell**.

We pack our bags. We say "goodbye, teacher!"

Mama is waiting at the gate.
===
## Telling Mama

"What did you do at school today?"

"I learnt a song. I played ampe. I shared my biscuit!"

Ask: Tell me one thing you did at **school** today.
===
## Tomorrow again

Tomorrow we come back. Our teacher. Our friends. Our classroom.

School is a happy place.

Say it: "I love my **school**!"
===
## Well done!

Teacher, friends, the bell, sharing, being kind, the rules.

Now you know all about **school**.

Ask: What is the best thing about your school?
