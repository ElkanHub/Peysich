## Names at home

Mama has a name. Daddy has a name.

Grandma has a name too.

Can you say Mama's name? Can you say Daddy's name?
===
## Listen

Shh. Be very quiet.

Put your hand on your **ear**.

What can you hear?
===
## Sounds outside

Listen. A car goes vroom.

A trotro horn goes peep peep!

Can you say "vroom"? Can you say "peep peep"?
===

Boom, ta, boom, ta!

Can you clap when I say boom?

## The trotro mate

The mate calls, "Accra! Accra! Circle!"

Loud and fast. "Accra, Accra!"

Can you **call** like the mate?
===
## The bell

A bell goes **ding**.

Ding, ding, ding!

The school bell says, "Come in!"
===
## Rain

Rain goes **pitter-patter** on the roof.

Pitter-patter, pitter-patter.

Tap your fingers on the table like rain.
===
## Loud and quiet

A drum is **loud**. Boom!

A whisper is **quiet**. Shh.

Say your name loud. Now say it quiet.
===
## Clap

Clap your hands. Clap, clap, clap.

Clap for Ama. Clap for Kofi.

Clap for **you**!
===
## Stamp

Stamp your feet. Stamp, stamp, stamp.

Stamp slow. Stamp fast.

What sound do your feet make?
===
## Mama's voice

Mama calls, "Ama! Come and eat!"

Ama knows **Mama's voice**.

Whose voice do you know? Who calls your name?
===
## My body makes sounds

Clap your hands.

Click your tongue.

Blow a kiss.

Hum a song. Mmm mmm mmm.
===
## Yes and no

Nod your head. That means **yes**.

Shake your head. That means **no**.

Do you like bananas? Nod or shake!
===
## Please and thank you

Kwame says **please**. "Water, please."

Kwame says **thank you**. "Thank you, Mama."

Can you say please? Can you say thank you?
===
## Good morning

In the morning we say **good morning**.

Good morning, Mama. Good morning, Daddy.

Good morning, sun!
===
## Good night

At night we say **good night**.

Good night, Ama. Good night, Kofi.

Shh. The house is quiet now.
===

## Think about it
1. What is your name? Can you say it with a clap?
2. What sound does a goat make? A dog? A cow?
3. Close your eyes and listen. What can you hear right now?
4. Whose voice do you know best?
5. What is a loud sound? What is a quiet sound?
