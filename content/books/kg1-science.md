## Plants are all around us

Look outside. Can you see a plant?

Grass is a plant. A mango tree is a plant. Maize is a plant.

Plants are **living** things. They grow, just like you!

Let us find out what a plant is made of.

## A plant has parts

You have parts: a head, arms, legs and feet.

A plant has parts too!

**Root. Stem. Leaf. Flower. Fruit.**

Each part has a job to do. Let us meet them one by one.

===
## The root

The **root** is under the ground. You cannot see it.

Roots hold the plant in the soil, like feet.

Roots drink water from the soil.

Pull up a weed with a grown-up. Look at the roots!

@image https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/2/2b/Pot_bound_roots_of_a_sampling.jpg/960px-Pot_bound_roots_of_a_sampling.jpg | The roots of a young plant | Suresh Khole · CC BY-SA 4.0 | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Pot_bound_roots_of_a_sampling.jpg
===
## What roots do

Ama pulls a cassava from the ground.

The cassava is a **root**! We eat it as fufu and banku.

Roots keep the plant standing when the wind blows.

Roots also drink water for the plant. Sip, sip!
===
## The stem

The **stem** stands up tall. It holds the leaves and flowers.

Water goes up the stem from the roots, like a straw.

On a tree, the stem is called the trunk.

Stand up straight. You are a stem!
===
## What stems do

Kofi sees a maize plant. Its stem is tall and green.

The stem lifts the leaves up to the sun.

A pawpaw tree has a thick stem. Grass has a thin stem.

Can you find a thick stem and a thin stem?
===
## The leaf

The **leaf** is flat and green.

Leaves make food for the plant using sunlight.

Leaves come in many shapes. Big, small, long, round.

Find a leaf. Feel it. Is it smooth or rough?

@image https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/f/f4/Leaf_1_web.jpg/960px-Leaf_1_web.jpg | A green leaf | Jon Sullivan · Public domain | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Leaf_1_web.jpg
===
## What leaves do

Leaves catch the sunlight. They are like little cooks.

They make food for the whole plant.

We eat some leaves too! Kontomire is a leaf. So is cabbage.

What leaves have you eaten?
===
## The flower

The **flower** is the pretty part. It has bright colours.

Red, yellow, pink, white, purple.

Bees and butterflies visit flowers.

Flowers help the plant make seeds and fruit.

@image https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/8/82/Hibiscus_rosa-sinensis_flower_2.JPG/960px-Hibiscus_rosa-sinensis_flower_2.JPG | A red hibiscus flower | B.navez · CC BY-SA 4.0 | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Hibiscus_rosa-sinensis_flower_2.JPG
===
## What flowers do

Abena smells a flower. It smells sweet!

A bee comes to drink from it. Buzz, buzz.

After the flower, a **fruit** grows in its place.

No flower, no fruit!
===
## The fruit

The **fruit** holds the seeds.

Mango, orange, pawpaw, banana, pineapple. All fruit!

Fruit is sweet and good to eat.

Cut open a mango. Can you find the big seed inside?

@image https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/7/74/Mangos_-_single_and_halved.jpg/960px-Mangos_-_single_and_halved.jpg | A mango, whole and cut open | Ivar Leidus · CC BY-SA 4.0 | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mangos_-_single_and_halved.jpg
===
## The seed

Inside the fruit is a **seed**.

A seed is a baby plant, sleeping.

Plant it in the soil. Give it water. It wakes up and grows!

A big mango tree starts as one small seed.
===
## From seed to plant

Kwame puts a bean in wet cotton wool.

Day 1: nothing. Day 3: a tiny **root** comes out.

Day 5: a little stem. Day 7: two small leaves!

Try it at home with a grown-up.

## All the parts together

Root at the bottom, in the soil.

Stem in the middle, standing tall.

Leaves on the stem, catching sun.

Flower at the top. Then **fruit** with seeds inside.

Point to each part on a plant you can see.

## Be a plant!

Crouch down. Your feet are the **roots**.

Stand up slowly. Your body is the stem.

Stretch out your arms. They are the leaves.

Open your hands wide. You are a flower!
===
## What do plants need?

Plants are alive. They need things to live, just like you.

You need food, water and air.

A plant needs **water, sunlight, air and soil**.

Let us look at each one.

@video dUBIQ1fTRzI | The Needs of a Plant (song for kids about 5 things plants need to live) | Harry Kindergarten Music
===
## Plants need water

Ama waters the garden every morning.

The roots drink the **water**. The plant stands up strong.

No water? The leaves droop and go brown.

When it rains, plants are happy!
===
## Plants need sunlight

Leaves need **sunlight** to make food.

Put a plant in a dark cupboard. It turns pale and weak.

Put it in the sun. It turns green and strong.

A sunflower even turns to face the sun!

@image https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/4/40/Sunflower_sky_backdrop.jpg/960px-Sunflower_sky_backdrop.jpg | A sunflower facing the sun | Fir0002 · GFDL 1.2 | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Sunflower_sky_backdrop.jpg
===
## Plants need air

Plants breathe! Not like you, but they take in **air** through their leaves.

Plants also give us clean air to breathe.

Trees are our friends. Thank you, trees!

Take a big breath. That air came from plants.
===
## Plants need soil

**Soil** is the ground where roots live.

Soil holds water and food for the plant.

Good soil is dark and soft.

Dig your fingers into some soil. How does it feel?
===
## A seedling grows

A baby plant is called a **seedling**.

It is small and soft. It needs care.

Water it. Keep goats away! Give it sun.

Soon it will be a big plant.

@image https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/2/23/Persea_americana_seedling_%28sprout_and_first_leaves%29.jpg/960px-Persea_americana_seedling_%28sprout_and_first_leaves%29.jpg | A young seedling with its first leaves | Eiku · CC BY-SA 4.0 | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Persea_americana_seedling_(sprout_and_first_leaves).jpg

## Plants we eat

Kofi's lunch comes from plants!

Rice is a **seed**. Kenkey is made from maize seeds.

Kontomire is a leaf. Yam is a root. Tomato is a fruit.

What plant parts did you eat today?
===
## Plants on the farm

Farmers grow plants for us to eat.

Maize, cassava, yam, plantain, cocoa.

They plant the seeds. They water them. They wait.

Then they harvest and bring food to the market.
===
## Plants give us more

Plants give us food. What else?

**Wood** for tables and chairs comes from tree stems.

Shade comes from leaves. Medicine comes from roots and leaves.

Cotton for your clothes comes from a plant too!
===
## Look after plants

Plants need us to be kind.

Do not pull off leaves for fun.

Do not step on seedlings.

Water a plant at home. Watch it grow. You are a plant helper!
===
## Grow your own

Ask a grown-up for a bean and a small tin.

Put soil in the tin. Push the bean in. Water it.

Put it by a window.

Count the days until a **leaf** comes out!
===
## Plant hunt

Go outside with a grown-up.

Find a **root**. Find a stem. Find a leaf.

Can you find a flower? Can you find a fruit?

Which was the hardest to find?
===
## Big and small plants

A mango tree is a big plant. Grass is a small plant.

Both have roots, stems and leaves.

Both need water, sun, air and soil.

Big or small, every plant has the same **parts**.
===
## Plant or not?

A stone is not a plant. It does not grow.

A goat is not a plant. It walks and eats.

A flower is a plant. It has roots and leaves and **grows**.

Is a chair a plant? Is a tree a plant?
===
## You know plants!

You know the parts: root, stem, leaf, flower, fruit.

You know what plants **need**: water, sunlight, air, soil.

Plants give us food, air and shade.

Go and say thank you to a tree!
