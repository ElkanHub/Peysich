## What is a computer?

A **computer** is a clever machine.

It can show pictures. It can play songs. It can help us learn and write.

Kofi's school has a computer. Have you seen one?

Let us find out what its parts are.

## A computer has parts

You have eyes to see, ears to hear and hands to touch.

A computer has parts too!

**Screen. Keyboard. Mouse. Speaker.**

Each part has a job. Let us meet them one by one.

===
## The screen

The **screen** is the part you look at.

It shows pictures, words, videos and games.

It is also called a monitor.

The screen is like a window. What can you see in it?

@image https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/7/77/Computer_Screen_Monitor.jpg/960px-Computer_Screen_Monitor.jpg | A computer screen, also called a monitor | U3211603 · CC BY-SA 4.0 | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Computer_Screen_Monitor.jpg
===
## What the screen does

Ama types her name. The letters show on the screen.

Kofi plays a song. The picture dances on the screen.

The screen **shows** us what the computer is doing.

Do not touch a monitor screen with wet or dirty hands!
===
## The keyboard

The **keyboard** has many little buttons called keys.

Each key has a letter or a number on it.

Press a key and the letter appears on the screen.

Can you find the letter of your name?

@image https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/0/0a/QWERTY_keyboard.jpg/960px-QWERTY_keyboard.jpg | A computer keyboard | MichaelMaggs · CC BY-SA 3.0 | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:QWERTY_keyboard.jpg
===
## What the keyboard does

The keyboard is for **typing**.

Abena presses A. Then M. Then A. "Ama" shows on the screen!

The long key at the bottom makes a space.

Press one key at a time. Tap, tap, tap.
===
## Letters and numbers

Look at the keyboard. The letters are not in ABC order!

Q, W, E, R, T, Y come first.

The **numbers** 1 to 9 and 0 are at the top.

Can you find the number 5? Can you find the letter K?
===
## The mouse

The **mouse** is small. It fits in your hand.

It is called a mouse because it has a long tail, like a real mouse!

Move it on the table. An arrow moves on the screen.

Squeak, squeak!

@image https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/1/1d/Computer_mouse_1_2015-02-28.JPG/960px-Computer_mouse_1_2015-02-28.JPG | A computer mouse | FASTILY · CC BY-SA 4.0 | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Computer_mouse_1_2015-02-28.JPG
===
## What the mouse does

The mouse moves the little **arrow** on the screen.

Move the mouse left. The arrow goes left.

Move it right. The arrow goes right.

The arrow points at what you want.
===
## Click!

The mouse has buttons. Press the left button. That is a **click**.

Click on a picture to open it.

Click on a game to play it.

Kwame clicks on a song. Music plays!
===
## Hold the mouse

Put your hand on the mouse, nice and flat.

Your first finger rests on the left button.

Move the mouse slowly. Do not lift it up.

Now **click** gently. Well done!
===
## The speaker

The **speaker** makes sound.

Songs, voices, the sound of a drum, a cow going moo.

Some speakers are inside the computer. Some sit next to it.

Cover your ears if it is too loud!

@image https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/8/86/Logitech_computer_loudspeakers.jpg/960px-Logitech_computer_loudspeakers.jpg | Computer speakers | Ralf Roletschek · GFDL 1.2 | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Logitech_computer_loudspeakers.jpg
===
## What the speaker does

Ama clicks on a counting song.

The **speaker** sings, "One, two, three!"

Kofi makes it louder. Abena makes it softer.

Which sound do you like best?

## The system unit

There is a box with the computer. It is the **system unit**.

It is the brain of the computer. It does the thinking.

The screen, keyboard and mouse all plug into it.

Do not open it. Do not put drinks on it!

@image https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/e/e2/Desktop_computer_system_diagram_%28English%29.svg/960px-Desktop_computer_system_diagram_%28English%29.svg.png | The parts of a desktop computer | kjarrett on Flickr · CC BY 2.0 | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Desktop_computer_system_diagram_(English).svg
===
## All the parts together

The **screen** shows.

The keyboard types.

The mouse points and clicks.

The speaker makes sound. The system unit thinks.

Point to each part on a real computer!
===
## The laptop

A **laptop** is a small computer that folds up.

The screen is on top. The keyboard is at the bottom.

It has a flat square called a touchpad instead of a mouse.

You can carry a laptop in a bag.

===
## The tablet and the phone

A **tablet** is a computer with only a screen.

You touch the screen with your finger. Tap, tap, swipe!

A phone is a tiny computer too.

Mama's phone can call, take photos and play songs.

@image https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/b/b5/Kids_with_Education_Tablet_Computers.jpg/960px-Kids_with_Education_Tablet_Computers.jpg | Children using tablets | Intel Free Press · CC BY-SA 2.0 | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Kids_with_Education_Tablet_Computers.jpg

## Turning it on

Find the **power button**. It has a little circle with a line.

Press it once. Lights come on. The screen wakes up.

Wait. The computer is getting ready.

Do not press it again and again!

## Turning it off

When you finish, you must **shut down**.

Ask a grown-up to help. Click the right button to turn it off.

Wait for the screen to go dark.

Never pull the plug while it is working!
===
## Computers help us learn

Kofi watches a video about plants.

Ama plays a counting game.

Abena listens to a story.

A computer is a **learning** helper. What would you like to learn?

## Computers help grown-ups

Papa types letters for his work.

The nurse keeps names on the computer.

The bank uses computers to count money. **GH₵** in, GH₵ out.

The teacher prints your report.
===
## The printer

A **printer** is a friend of the computer.

Type words on the screen. Click print. The paper comes out!

Zzzz... zzzz... Here is your page.

Printers need paper and ink.
===
## Keep it clean

Computers do not like dirt or water.

Wash your hands before you use one.

No food or drink near the keyboard.

If you spill water on it, tell a **grown-up** at once!
===
## Be gentle

Press the keys softly. Do not bang them.

Move the mouse slowly. Do not pull its tail.

Do not poke the screen with a pencil.

A computer is not a toy. Be **gentle**.
===
## Sit well

Sit up straight on your chair.

Keep the screen a little way from your face. Not too close!

Your feet flat on the floor.

After a while, stand up and **rest** your eyes.
===
## Ask first

Always ask a grown-up before you use a computer.

Only open things a grown-up says you can.

If something scary shows on the screen, look away and **tell** a grown-up.

Computers are for sharing. Take turns!
===
## Which part?

Kofi wants to hear a song. Which part makes sound?

Ama wants to type her name. Which part does she use?

Abena wants to see a picture. Which part shows it?

Say the **part** for each one.
===
## Computer or not?

A radio is not a computer. It only plays sound.

A chair is not a computer.

A laptop is a **computer**. A tablet is a computer.

Is a pencil a computer? Is a phone a computer?
===
