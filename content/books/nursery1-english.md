## Let us sing

Words can **sing**. Words can **clap**.

In this book we will say rhymes, clap our names, and listen for sounds.

Open your ears. Open your mouth. Here we go!

> Ask: What is your favourite song?
===
## Twinkle, twinkle

Twinkle, twinkle, little **star**,

How I wonder what you are.

Open and close your hands like a star. Sing it softly.

## Rhyming words

**Star** and **are**. They end the same way.

Star... are. Hear it?

Words that end the same are **rhymes**.

> Ask: Cat, hat. Do they rhyme?
===
## Clap your name

Say your name. Clap each part.

A-ma. Clap, clap. Two claps!

Ko-fi. Clap, clap. Two claps!

A-be-na. Clap, clap, clap. **Three** claps!

> Ask: How many claps in your name?

## Clap the food

Ken-key. Clap, clap.

Ban-a-na. Clap, clap, clap.

Rice. **One** clap!

> Ask: Can you clap "man-go"?
===
## The drum

A drum goes **boom**. Boom, boom, boom.

Tap the beat on your knees. Slow... then fast!

Words have beats too, like a drum.

## Loud and soft

Say "boom" **loud**. BOOM!

Say "boom" **soft**. boom.

Say it like a mouse. Say it like a lion.

> Ask: Can you say your name like a lion?
===
## First sounds

Every word starts with a **sound**.

Mmm-mango. The first sound is **mmm**.

Say it: mmm... mango.

## A is for Ama

Names start with sounds too.

**A**-ma. **K**-ofi. **A**-bena. **K**-wame.

Say your name slowly. What sound is first?

> Ask: Whose name starts like yours?

## Animal sounds

Goat says **meh**. Hen says **cluck**.

Dog says **woof**. Cat says **meow**.

Say each one. Which is your favourite?

> Ask: What sound does a cockerel make?
===
## Head, shoulders

Head, shoulders, knees and toes, knees and toes.

Touch each part as you sing.

Go slow. Then go **fast**!

## Rhyme time: at

C-at. H-at. M-at. B-at.

They all end in **at**.

Cat, hat, mat, bat. Say them fast!

> Ask: Does "rat" rhyme with "cat"?
===
## Rhyme time: op

H-op. T-op. M-op. P-op.

They all end in **op**.

Hop like a frog. Hop, hop, hop!
===
## Finish the rhyme

I say the start. You say the end.

"Twinkle, twinkle, little..." **star**!

"Baa baa black..." **sheep**!

> Ask: "One, two, buckle my..."
===
## Clap and stamp

Clap for **one** beat. Stamp for **two**.

Clap... stamp, stamp. Clap... stamp, stamp.

Now say "Kofi" and clap it. Say "Abena" and stamp it.
===
## Quiet sounds

Shhh. Listen.

What can you hear? A bird? A car? A fan?

Some sounds are **quiet**. Some are **loud**.

> Ask: Can you make a very quiet sound?
===
## Say it slowly

Say "trotro" slowly. **Tro... tro.**

Two parts. Clap, clap.

Say "market" slowly. **Mar... ket.** Clap, clap.
===
## Silly sounds

Make a sound like a motorbike. Vroom!

Make a sound like rain. Pitter-patter.

Make a sound like a goat. Meh!

Your mouth can make **so many** sounds.
===
## Rhymes on the way

On the way to school, sing a rhyme.

In the trotro, clap a name.

At the market, listen for sounds.

Rhymes go **everywhere** with you.
===
## My name song

Ama, Ama, where are you?

Here I am, here I am, how do you do?

Sing it with **your** name!

> Ask: Shall we sing it with Grandma's name?
===
## Listen for the sound

I will say three words. Which starts with **b**?

Ball. Cat. Mango.

**Ball**! B-b-ball. Well done.

> Ask: Which starts with m: dog, mango, hen?
===
## Make a rhyme

Say a word. Say a word that sounds like it.

Hen... pen. Dog... log. Fish... dish.

Silly rhymes are the **best** rhymes.
===
## Sing again

Sing Twinkle again. Sing it to a baby.

Sing it to a cat. Sing it to a goat!

The more you sing, the more words you **know**.
===
## I know rhymes!

I can clap my name. I can find a rhyme.

I can hear the first sound in a word.

I can sing loud and sing soft. **Well done, me!**

> Ask: Which rhyme shall we sing tomorrow?
