## A tablet and a phone

This is a **tablet**. This is a **phone**.

They have a screen. The screen shows pictures, songs and games.

You can touch the screen to make things happen!

Ask: Who in your house has a phone?

## Ask first

Before you touch a tablet or phone, **ask** a grown-up.

"May I use the tablet, please?"

Wait for the answer. If they say no, that is okay.

## Clean hands first

Screens do not like sticky fingers!

**Wash your hands** before you touch the tablet. Dry them well.

Ask: Are your hands clean and dry?

@image https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/8/83/School_children_washing_hands_in_Ghana.jpg/960px-School_children_washing_hands_in_Ghana.jpg | Clean hands before we touch the screen | Mac-Gbathy · CC BY-SA 4.0 | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:School_children_washing_hands_in_Ghana.jpg

## Hold it with two hands

A tablet is not a toy. It can break.

Hold it with **two hands**. Sit down. Keep it away from the edge of the table.

Ask: Show me how to hold a tablet.

@image https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/4/40/Child_using_a_low_cost_tablet_computer_in_Thailand.png/960px-Child_using_a_low_cost_tablet_computer_in_Thailand.png | Hold the tablet with two hands | He!ko · CC BY-SA 3.0 | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Child_using_a_low_cost_tablet_computer_in_Thailand.png

## Gentle hands

Soft, soft, soft. The screen is like glass.

We do not bang it. We do not throw it. We do not drop it.

We use **gentle** hands.

@video Zd59xrsYCNE | Learn Gentle Hands Song for Children Social and Emotional Skills for Toddlers by Patty Shukla | Patty Shukla Kids TV - Children's songs
===
## Tap

Put out one finger. Touch the screen softly. Lift it up.

That is a **tap**!

Tap, tap, tap. Like a little bird pecking.

Ask: Can you tap the table with one finger?
===
## Tap to open

Pictures on the screen are called **icons**.

**Tap** an icon. It opens! A game. A song. A story.

Ask: What picture would you like to tap?
===
## One finger, one tap

Only one finger. Only one tap.

If you tap and tap and tap, the tablet gets confused!

**Tap** once. Then wait.
===
## Swipe

Put one finger on the screen. Slide it across. Lift.

That is a **swipe**!

Swipe like you are wiping a window.

Ask: Can you swipe your hand across the mat?
===
## Swipe to turn the page

In a story app, **swipe** to see the next page.

Swipe left: go forward. Swipe right: go back.

Just like turning a page in a real book!
===
## Swipe up and down

**Swipe** up to see more. Swipe down to go back up.

Up, up, up. Down, down, down.

Ask: Which way is up? Point to it.
===
## Drawing with a finger

Kofi opens a drawing app. He swipes his finger.

A red line! A blue circle! A yellow sun!

Ask: What would you draw?

## The home button

Lost? Do not worry.

Press the **home button**. It takes you back to the start.

Some tablets have a round button. Some have a swipe from the bottom.

Ask: Where is the home button on this phone?

@image https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/f/fd/IPhone_5S_Home_Button.jpg/960px-IPhone_5S_Home_Button.jpg | A phone's home button | randychiu · CC BY 2.0 | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:IPhone_5S_Home_Button.jpg
===
## Home means start

The **home screen** is where all the icons live.

Press home. See the icons. Pick one. Tap!
===
## Something strange?

Sometimes a strange picture pops up. Sometimes words you do not know.

Do not tap it. **Stop**. Call a grown-up.

"Mama, what is this?"
===
## Do not tap "buy"

Some games say "Buy!" or "Pay!".

Never tap that. It costs Mama's money.

**Ask** a grown-up first. Always.
===
## Keep it dry

Water and tablets do not mix!

No tablet near the bath. No tablet near your cup of water.

Keep it **dry** and safe.
===
## Charging

The tablet gets tired. The battery goes low.

A grown-up plugs in the **charger**. Now it can rest and fill up.

Ask: Where does the tablet charge in your house?

## Not too close

Hold the screen away from your face. Not too close!

Sit up straight. Keep the room bright.

This keeps your **eyes** happy.

Ask: Show me how far away the screen should be.
===
## Not too long

A little screen time is fun. Too much is not good.

When the grown-up says "time is up", we **stop**.

We say "okay" and put it down. No crying!
===
## Rest your eyes

After the tablet, look far away. Look at a tree. Look at the sky.

Blink, blink. Your **eyes** need a rest.

Ask: What can you see far away right now?
===
## Learning on a tablet

Tablets can help us learn! Letters. Numbers. Songs. Stories.

Abena taps the letter A. "A is for apple!"

Ask: What would you like to **learn** on a tablet?

## Sharing the tablet

One tablet. Two children.

Kofi has a turn. Then Ama has a turn.

We **share**. We wait. We do not grab.

Ask: How do you feel when you wait for your turn?
===
## Talking to a grown-up

The tablet is fun. But people are better!

Show Mama your drawing. Tell Papa about the song.

**Talk** about what you did.
===
## Playing outside

After the tablet, go and play!

Run. Jump. Kick a ball. Climb a tree.

Your **body** needs to move too.

Ask: What will you play outside today?
===
## Putting it away

Finished? Press **home**. Turn off the screen.

Give it back to the grown-up with two hands.

"Thank you for letting me use it."
===
## Be safe online

Some apps talk to other people. We never tell them our name or where we live.

If someone you do not know talks to you, **tell a grown-up**.

===
## The tablet rules

1. **Ask** first.
2. Clean, **gentle** hands.
3. **Tap** once. **Swipe** softly.
4. Something strange? **Stop** and tell.
5. Time up? Put it **down**.

Ask: Can you say the rules with me?
===
## Practise together

Let us try. Ask: "May I use the tablet?"

Tap an icon. Swipe a page. Press home.

Give it back with two hands. **Well done**!
===
## Well done!

Tap. Swipe. Home. Ask.

Now you know how to use a tablet **gently** and **safely**.

Ask: What was your favourite thing to learn?
