## This is me

This is Kofi. This is Kofi's **body**.

Head, hands, feet. All of it is Kofi!

Pat your tummy. This is you!

## My head

This is my **head**. It is at the top.

Pat, pat, pat your head.

Can you nod your head? Yes, yes, yes!
===
## My hair

On my head is my **hair**.

Touch your hair. Is it soft?

Ama's hair is in braids. Kofi's hair is short.
===
## My face

My **face** is on the front of my head.

Eyes, nose, mouth, all on my face.

Make a happy face. Now make a funny face!
===
## My eyes

I have two **eyes**.

My eyes can see.

I see Mama. I see the sun. I see you!

Can you point to your eyes?

@image https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/0/03/Female_eye_close_up.jpg/960px-Female_eye_close_up.jpg | An eye can see | Grauvision · CC BY 4.0 | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Female_eye_close_up.jpg
===
## Open and shut

Open your eyes. I can see!

Shut your eyes. It is dark.

Open, shut. Open, shut. Blink, blink!
===
## What can you see?

Look around. What can your **eyes** see?

A chair? A cup? A door?

Point to something red.
===
## My ears

I have two **ears**, one on each side.

My ears can hear.

I hear a drum. I hear a bird. I hear Mama's voice.

Can you point to your ears?
===
## Listen

Put your hands over your ears. Quiet.

Take them off. Loud!

What can your **ears** hear now?
===
## My nose

This is my **nose**. It is in the middle of my face.

My nose can smell.

Mmm, I smell soup. I smell oranges.

Can you point to your nose?
===
## Breathe

Breathe in through your nose. Sniff!

Breathe out. Whoosh!

Your **nose** helps you breathe.
===
## My mouth

This is my **mouth**.

My mouth can eat. My mouth can talk. My mouth can sing!

Open wide. Aaah!
===
## My teeth

Inside my mouth are my **teeth**.

Teeth chew food. Crunch, crunch.

Show me your teeth. Big smile!
===
## Kenkey time

Abena eats kenkey with her **mouth**.

Her teeth chew. Her tongue tastes. Mmm, yummy!

What do you like to eat?

===
## Head, shoulders, knees and toes

Touch your head. Touch your shoulders.

Touch your knees. Touch your toes!

Sing it and do it!

## My hands

I have two **hands**.

My hands can hold. My hands can clap.

Clap, clap, clap!

Can you show me your hands?
===
## My fingers

On each hand I have five **fingers**.

Wiggle, wiggle, wiggle.

Can you wiggle your fingers?
===
## Hands can carry

Kwame carries a pot with his **hands**.

Hands can carry. Hands can hold. Hands can help.

What can you carry?

## Wash your hands

Hands get dirty. So we **wash** them.

Water, soap, rub, rub, rub. Rinse!

Clean hands before we eat.

@image https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/9/93/Child_washing_hands_closeup.jpg/960px-Child_washing_hands_closeup.jpg | Rub, rub, rub | Shixart1985 · CC BY 2.0 | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Child_washing_hands_closeup.jpg
===
## My arms

My **arms** join my hands to my body.

Arms go up. Arms go down.

Arms go round and round!
===
## My legs

I have two **legs**.

Legs are long. Legs are strong.

Stand up. Stamp your legs!
===
## My feet

At the end of my legs are my **feet**.

My feet can walk. My feet can run. My feet can jump!

Can you point to your feet?
===
## My toes

On each foot I have five **toes**.

Wiggle your toes. Wiggle, wiggle!

Can you count your toes with a grown-up?
===
## Feet can run

Kofi and his friends run with their **feet**.

Run, run, run! Kick the ball!

Can you run on the spot?

@image https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/8/86/Children_playing_football_with_their_barefoot_in_northern_Ghana.jpg/960px-Children_playing_football_with_their_barefoot_in_northern_Ghana.jpg | Feet can run and kick | Tahiru Rajab · CC BY-SA 4.0 | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Children_playing_football_with_their_barefoot_in_northern_Ghana.jpg
===
## My tummy

This is my **tummy**.

Food goes into my tummy. Yum!

Pat your tummy. Is it full or empty?
===
## My heart

Put your hand on your chest. Thump, thump.

That is your **heart**. It beats all day and all night.

Run on the spot. Now feel it. Faster!
===
## All of me

Head, eyes, ears, nose, mouth.

Hands, arms, legs, feet.

All of this is **me**!

## Take care of me

Wash your body. Eat good food. Drink water.

Sleep at night. Play in the day.

Keep your **body** strong!

## Point and say

Point to your head. Point to your eyes.

Point to your ears. Point to your nose.

Point to your hands. Point to your feet.

Say each **word** as you point!
===
## Well done!

You know your body.

Eyes see. Ears hear. Nose smells. Mouth eats.

Hands hold. Feet walk.

Clap for **you**!
