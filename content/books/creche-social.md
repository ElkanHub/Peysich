## My family

This is Ama's **family**.

Mama, Daddy, brother, sister, Grandma, Grandpa.

A family lives together. A family loves together.

Who is in your family?
===
## Mother

This is **Mama**.

Mama holds me. Mama feeds me. Mama sings to me.

I love Mama.

Can you say "Mama"?

Daddy lifts me up high. Daddy walks with me.

I love Daddy.

## Daddy comes home

Daddy comes home from work.

"Daddy!" Kofi runs to the door.

Daddy says, "Hello, my boy!"

Who do you run to?
===
## The people in my family

Mother, father, sister, brother.

Grandma, grandpa, baby too.

## Big sister

This is Abena. She is Ama's **big sister**.

Abena holds Ama's hand on the road.

Big sister looks after little sister.

Do you have a sister?
===
## Big brother

This is Kwame. He is Kofi's **big brother**.

Kwame shares his ball with Kofi.

Do you have a brother?

@image https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/0/02/Two_siblings_on_their_way_home_after_school.jpg/960px-Two_siblings_on_their_way_home_after_school.jpg | Brother and sister walk home together | Kwameghana · CC BY-SA 4.0 | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Two_siblings_on_their_way_home_after_school.jpg
===
## Baby

A **baby** is the smallest in the family.

Baby cries. Baby sleeps. Baby laughs.

We are gentle with baby. Shh.
===
## Grandma

This is **Grandma**. She is Mama's mama.

Grandma tells stories. Grandma gives me groundnuts.

I love Grandma.

@image https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/8/87/Grandmother_and_grandchild.jpg/960px-Grandmother_and_grandchild.jpg | Grandma and me | Sophiestolle · CC BY-SA 4.0 | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Grandmother_and_grandchild.jpg
===
## Grandpa

This is **Grandpa**. He is Daddy's daddy.

Grandpa sits under the tree. Grandpa sings old songs.

I sit with Grandpa.

Do you have a grandpa?
===
## Grandma's face

Grandma's face has lines. Grandma's hair is grey.

Grandma has lived a long, long time.

We say, "Good morning, **Grandma**." We greet our elders.

@image https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/3/32/African_grandmother.jpg/960px-African_grandmother.jpg | Grandma | AUGUSTINE KIAGO · CC BY-SA 4.0 | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:African_grandmother.jpg
===
## Aunties and uncles

Mama's sister is my **auntie**.

Daddy's brother is my **uncle**.

They come to visit. We say, "Welcome!"
===
## Love

My family **loves** me.

They hug me. They feed me. They keep me safe.

I love my family too.

## We eat together

The family sits down.

Mama shares the rice. Daddy shares the stew.

We **eat** together. Yum!
===
## Mama cooks

Mama **cooks** the food.

Stir, stir, stir the pot. Mmm, it smells good.

What does Mama cook at your house?

## I can help

I am small. But I can **help**.

I can carry my cup. I can pick up my toys.

What can you do to help?
===
## Sweep

Mama sweeps the floor. Swish, swish.

Ama helps. Swish, swish.

Can you **sweep**? Swish, swish, swish!

@image https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/5/59/African_woman_sweeping_Her_Home.jpg/960px-African_woman_sweeping_Her_Home.jpg | Swish, swish | Nosakhare Okuons · CC BY-SA 4.0 | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:African_woman_sweeping_Her_Home.jpg
===
## Tidy up

Toys on the floor. Oh no!

Kofi puts the toys in the box.

**Tidy** up, tidy up. All done!
===
## Carry water

Kwame carries a small bucket of water.

Careful, careful. Do not spill!

Can you **carry** a cup without spilling?
===
## Say thank you

Mama gives me food. I say **thank you**.

Daddy gives me a hug. I say thank you.

Who can you say thank you to today?
===
## Say sorry

Kofi pushes Ama. Ama cries.

Kofi says, "**Sorry**, Ama."

Ama says, "It is okay."

Hug, hug. Friends again.
===
## Share

One orange. Two children.

Abena breaks it in half. One for Abena, one for Ama.

We **share**.
===
## Wait your turn

Kwame goes first. Then Kofi.

Kofi waits. His **turn** will come.

Waiting is hard. But Kofi can do it!
===
## Going to the market

Mama goes to the **market**. Ama goes with her.

Mama buys tomatoes for GH₵5.

Ama holds the bag. Ama is helping!
===
## Family at night

The sun goes down.

Mama, Daddy, Grandma, all at home.

We say **good night**. We sleep safe.
===
## Family at church and mosque

On Sunday some families go to church.

On Friday some families go to the mosque.

Families **pray** together. Families sing together.
===
## Everyone is different

Ama's family is big. Kofi's family is small.

Some children live with Grandma. Some live with Auntie.

Every family is **special**.
===
## My family loves me

Mama, Daddy, sister, brother, Grandma, Grandpa.

They love me. I love them.

I **help** at home.

Give your family a big hug!
