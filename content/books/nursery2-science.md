## Day and night

Every day the sky changes.

Bright and blue. Then dark with stars.

This is **day** and **night**. Let us see what happens!
===
## The sun comes up

Early in the morning, the sun comes up.

The sky turns pink, then orange, then blue.

This is **sunrise**. A new day begins!

Ask: Where does the sun come up near your house?

## Cock-a-doodle-doo!

The cockerel wakes up first.

"Cock-a-doodle-doo!" he shouts. Wake up, everyone! It is time to get up.

Say it: **morning**.

## Good morning!

Kofi opens his eyes. He stretches. He yawns.

"Good **morning**, Mama!"

Ask: What do you say when you wake up?

## Morning jobs

In the morning we wash our face. We brush our teeth.

We put on our uniform. We eat our porridge.

Ask: What do you do first in the **morning**?
===
## The town wakes up

The street is busy. Trotros beep. Sellers call out.

Children walk to school. The **morning** is cool.

@image https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/b/b8/Early_morning_view_in_the_streets_of_Tamale_%28Northern_Ghana%29_01.jpg | Early morning in the streets of Tamale | Sir Amugi · CC BY-SA 4.0 | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Early_morning_view_in_the_streets_of_Tamale_(Northern_Ghana)_01.jpg
===
## The sun

The **sun** is a big ball of light. It is very far away.

It gives us light. It gives us warmth.

Never look straight at the sun. It is too bright!

Ask: Is the sun hot or cold?
===
## Daytime is bright

When the sun is up, it is **day**.

We can see everything. The trees, the road, the sky.

We play. We learn. We work.

Ask: What can you see outside right now?
===
## The sun goes up high

At midday the sun is high, high up.

It is very hot. Your shadow is short!

Say it: **midday**.

Ask: Where is your shadow when the sun is high?
===
## Afternoon

After lunch, it is **afternoon**.

School is over. Ama plays with her friends.

The sun starts to slide down the sky.

Ask: What do you do in the afternoon?
===
## Shadows

Stand in the sun. Look down. A dark shape follows you!

That is your **shadow**. The sun makes it.

Ask: Can you make your shadow wave?
===
## The sun goes down

Now the sun is low. The sky turns orange and red.

This is **sunset**. Day is ending.

Ask: What colours do you see at sunset?

@image https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/c/c8/Sunset_at_Labadi_Beach_Accra.jpg | Sunset at Labadi Beach, Accra | Danieljatuat · CC BY-SA 4.0 | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Sunset_at_Labadi_Beach_Accra.jpg
===
## Evening

The sun is gone. The sky is grey, then dark.

This is **evening**. Mama cooks. Papa comes home.

We eat our supper together.

Ask: What do you eat for supper?
===
## Lights come on

It is dark now. Click! The lights come on.

Street lamps glow. Houses shine. Cars have bright eyes.

Say it: **dark**.

@image https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/c/c7/Landscape_view_of_the_night_sky_with_street_lights_in_Kumasi.jpg/960px-Landscape_view_of_the_night_sky_with_street_lights_in_Kumasi.jpg | Street lights at night in Kumasi | Maame1Yaa · CC BY-SA 4.0 | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Landscape_view_of_the_night_sky_with_street_lights_in_Kumasi.jpg
===
## Night

Now it is **night**. The sky is black.

It is cool. It is quiet. Crickets sing.

Ask: What sounds do you hear at night?
===
## The moon

Look up! A light in the dark sky.

It is the **moon**. It is round and silver.

The moon does not make its own light. The sun shines on it!

@image https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/d/dd/Full_Moon_Luc_Viatour.jpg/960px-Full_Moon_Luc_Viatour.jpg | A full moon | Luc Viatour · CC BY-SA 3.0 | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Full_Moon_Luc_Viatour.jpg
===
## The moon changes

Some nights the moon is a big round circle.

Some nights it is thin, like a banana!

The **moon** changes shape a little every night.

Ask: What shape is the moon tonight?

@image https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/4/4e/Crescent_moon_-_Hilal.jpg/960px-Crescent_moon_-_Hilal.jpg | A thin crescent moon | Zeynel Cebeci · CC BY-SA 4.0 | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Crescent_moon_-_Hilal.jpg
===
## The stars

Tiny lights, twinkling in the dark.

These are the **stars**. There are more than you can count!

Twinkle, twinkle, little star.

@image https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/f/f0/Night_Sky_Stars_Trees_02.jpg/960px-Night_Sky_Stars_Trees_02.jpg | Stars in the night sky | Michael J. Bennett · CC BY-SA 3.0 | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Night_Sky_Stars_Trees_02.jpg
===
## Sing about the sky

Sun, moon and stars. Let us sing about them!

Point up when you hear **sun**. Point up when you hear **moon**.

## Where did the sun go?

At night, the sun has not gone away.

It is shining on the other side of the world!

When it is **night** here, it is **day** there.
===
## Night jobs

At night we bath. We put on our pyjamas.

We brush our teeth. We say goodnight.

Ask: What do you do before **bed**?
===
## Time to sleep

Abena is tired. She lies on her bed.

She closes her eyes. Zzz, zzz.

At **night** we sleep so we can grow.

Ask: Who tucks you into bed?
===
## Animals at night

Some animals sleep at night, just like you.

But owls and bats wake up at **night**! They fly in the dark.

Ask: Do you know any animal that likes the night?
===
## Animals in the day

Hens, goats and butterflies are up in the **day**.

They eat and play when the sun is out.

At night they sleep too.
===
## Day things

Sun. Bright sky. School. Play. Shadows.

These are **day** things.

Ask: Name one thing you only do in the day.
===
## Night things

Moon. Stars. Dark sky. Pyjamas. Sleep.

These are **night** things.

Ask: Name one thing you only do at night.
===
## Round and round

Morning, afternoon, evening, night.

Then the sun comes up again. A new **day**!

Round and round it goes, every single day.
===
## A day in order

Say them in order with me:

**Morning**, **afternoon**, **evening**, **night**.

Clap once for each one!

Ask: Which part of the day is it right now?
===
## Look at the sky tonight

Tonight, go outside with a grown-up.

Look up. Can you see the **moon**? Can you count five **stars**?

Ask: Is the sky cloudy or clear?
===
## Well done!

Now you know about **day** and **night**.

The sun for the day. The moon and stars for the night.

Ask: What is your favourite part of the day?
===
