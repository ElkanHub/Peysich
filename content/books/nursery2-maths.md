## Shapes are everywhere

Look around you. Shapes are everywhere!

A plate. A window. A door. A ball.

Today we will learn four **shapes**.

Ask: Can you see a round thing in the room?
===
## This is a circle

A **circle** is round. It has no corners.

Run your finger round and round. It never stops!

Say it: **circle**.

@image https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/a/a0/Circle_-_black_simple.svg/960px-Circle_-_black_simple.svg.png | A circle is round, with no corners | Dakdada · Public domain | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Circle_-_black_simple.svg
===
## Draw a circle in the air

Lift your finger high.

Round and round, like a wheel. Round and round, like the sun.

You just drew a **circle**!

Ask: Can you draw a big circle? Now a tiny one?
===
## A ball is a circle

Kofi kicks his football. Roll, roll, roll!

Look at the ball. It is round like a **circle**.

Ask: What else rolls? Can a square roll?

@image https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/5/50/Close_up_soccer_ball.png | A football is round like a circle | Nemo · CC0 | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Close_up_soccer_ball.png
===
## Circles at home

A plate for your kenkey. A cup for your water. A coin in Mama's hand.

All of them are **circles**!

Ask: Find a circle in the kitchen. What is it?
===
## The clock is a circle

Tick, tock. Tick, tock.

The clock on the wall is a **circle**. The hands go round and round.

Ask: What shape is the clock? Where is the clock in your house?

@image https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/4/46/Wall_clock_in_morning_time.jpg/960px-Wall_clock_in_morning_time.jpg | A round wall clock | Garonzi Stefania · CC BY-SA 4.0 | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Wall_clock_in_morning_time.jpg

## This is a square

A **square** has four sides. All four sides are the same.

It has four corners. Count them: one, two, three, four!

Say it: **square**.

@image https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/d/dd/Square_-_black_simple.svg/960px-Square_-_black_simple.svg.png | A square has four equal sides | Wikimedia Commons contributor · Public domain | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Square_-_black_simple.svg
===
## Draw a square in the air

Across. Down. Back. Up.

Four straight lines. Four sharp corners.

You drew a **square**!

Ask: Can you count the corners on your square?
===
## Squares at home

A biscuit. A floor tile. A little window.

Abena finds a **square** on her mat.

Ask: Can you find a square in your room? Touch its corners.
===
## A square has corners

A circle has no corners. A **square** has four.

Touch a corner. It is pointy!

Ask: Which has corners, the ball or the biscuit?
===
## This is a triangle

A **triangle** has three sides. One, two, three.

It has three corners too. It looks like a pointy hat!

Say it: **triangle**.

@image https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/7/7e/Triangle_Orange.svg/960px-Triangle_Orange.svg.png | A triangle has three sides and three corners | Константине12591 · Public domain | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Triangle_Orange.svg
===
## Draw a triangle in the air

Up to the top. Down to the corner. Back home.

Three lines. Three corners.

You drew a **triangle**!

Ask: Point to the top of your triangle.
===
## Triangles in town

A roof on a house. A slice of pie. A road sign on the way to the market.

Kwame sees a **triangle** on the trotro road!

Ask: What triangles do you see on the way to school?
===
## Make a triangle with your hands

Put your two hands together. Fingers touch at the top. Thumbs touch at the bottom.

Look through the hole. It is a **triangle**!

Ask: Can you make a big triangle with your arms?
===
## This is a rectangle

A **rectangle** has four sides too. But two sides are long and two sides are short.

It is like a square that got stretched!

Say it: **rectangle**.

@image https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/8/86/Yellow_rectangle_horizontal.svg/960px-Yellow_rectangle_horizontal.svg.png | A rectangle has two long sides and two short sides | Hakutora · Public domain | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Yellow_rectangle_horizontal.svg
===
## Draw a rectangle in the air

A long line across. A short line down. Long line back. Short line up.

You drew a **rectangle**!

Ask: Which sides are long? Which sides are short?
===
## The door is a rectangle

Ama opens the door. Tall and straight.

The door is a **rectangle**. So is your book!

Ask: What shape is the door? What shape is this phone?

@image https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/4/42/Wooden_Door_in_Ghana.jpg/960px-Wooden_Door_in_Ghana.jpg | A wooden door is a rectangle | Fanti Salms · CC BY-SA 4.0 | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Wooden_Door_in_Ghana.jpg
===
## Rectangles at home

Your bed. Your table. A mobile phone. A cedi note.

So many **rectangles**!

Ask: Find three rectangles in your house.
===
## Square or rectangle?

Both have four sides. Both have four corners.

A **square** has sides that are all the same.

A **rectangle** has long sides and short sides.

Ask: Is a door a square or a rectangle?
===
## Shapes on the trotro

Look at the trotro! Round wheels are **circles**.

The windows are **rectangles**. The big windscreen too.

Ask: How many circles can you count on a trotro?

@image https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/6/64/Trotro_Accra_1.jpg/960px-Trotro_Accra_1.jpg | A trotro has round wheels and rectangle windows | NanaYawBotar · CC BY-SA 4.0 | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Trotro_Accra_1.jpg
===
## Shapes at the market

Mama goes to the market. Round oranges. Round tomatoes.

A square box of sugar. A long rectangle table.

Ask: What shape is an orange? What shape is a box?
===
## Shapes in the sky

The sun is a big **circle**. The moon can be a circle too.

A kite is a **triangle** flying high!

Ask: What shape is the sun?
===

## Shape hunt: circle

Ready? Go!

Find one **circle** in your house. Bring it. Show it.

Say: "This is a circle!"
===
## Shape hunt: square

Now find one **square**.

A tile? A biscuit? A small box?

Count its four corners out loud.
===
## Shape hunt: triangle

Now find a **triangle**.

Hard to find? Fold a paper corner to corner. There is your triangle!

Ask: Can you make a triangle with three sticks?
===
## Shape hunt: rectangle

Now find a **rectangle**.

A book? A door? The TV?

Say: "Two long sides, two short sides."
===
## Make a house with shapes

A **square** for the wall. A **triangle** for the roof.

A **rectangle** for the door. A **circle** for the sun.

Draw it. It is a house!
===
## Well done!

Circle, square, triangle, rectangle.

Now you know four **shapes**. You can find them everywhere.

Ask: Which shape is your favourite? Why?
