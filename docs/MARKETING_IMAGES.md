fine, keep the aspect ratio.

## Notes
- Faces: varied, natural, no exaggerated features; nobody in distress; no brand
  clothing.
- Phones: always a plain lit rectangle — the real app screenshots do the UI work.
- If a generator insists on a third colour, ask for "strictly duotone, wine on
  kraft, no other hues". Tiny paper-white highlights are fine.
- Keep the grain: it's the texture that makes the language, and it hides
  compression well, so files stay small.
