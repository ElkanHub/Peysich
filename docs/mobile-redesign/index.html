Grey/wine blocks labelled img: … are images still to be generated — see IMAGE_PROMPTS.md. Lists — the part that collapsed

The DataTable turns into "Header: value" cards below 768px. That is the bland, cluttered look your friend saw. Every list becomes rows: avatar, title, one subtitle line, a pill if something matters, a chevron. Filters become a snap row of chips plus one Filter button that opens a bottom sheet. Stats and link-button rows above lists are cut to one line.
